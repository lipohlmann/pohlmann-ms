\subsection{Spherical Harmonics}\label{sec:spherical-harmonics}
The spherical harmonics themselves are defined as~\cite{bellNuclearReactorTheory1970}:
\begin{equation}
  Y_{lm}\anglevars=\sqrt{\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}}P_l^m\left(\cos\varphi\right)e^{im\theta}
\end{equation}
where $P_l^m$ is the associate Legendre polynomial and $\theta\in [0,2\pi]$, $\varphi\in [0,\pi]$.
