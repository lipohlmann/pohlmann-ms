The steady-state, monoenergetic \gls{bte} can be generically written as:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:general-bte}
  \ohat\cdot\nabla\psigeneral+\Sigma_t\psigeneral=\Qgeneral.
\end{equation}
Assuming $\Sigma_t>0$, solving for the angular flux by inverting the loss term gives:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:solve-for-psi}
  \psigeneral=\frac{1}{\Sigma_t}\left(\Qgeneral-\ohat\cdot\nabla\psigeneral\right)
\end{equation}
Substituting \cref{eq:solve-for-psi} into \cref{eq:general-bte}'s streaming term gives:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:saaf-partial}
  \ohat\cdot\nabla\left(\frac{1}{\Sigma_t}\left(\Qgeneral-\ohat\cdot\nabla\psigeneral\right)\right)+\Sigma_t\psigeneral=\Qgeneral
\end{equation}
Finally, using the linearity of the gradient operator, we arrive at the \gls{saaf} form of the \gls{bte}~\cite{liscum-powellFiniteElementSolution2002}:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:saaf-general}
  -\ohat\cdot\nabla\left(\frac{1}{\Sigma_t}\ohat\cdot\nabla\psigeneral\right)+\Sigma_t\psigeneral=\Qgeneral-\ohat\cdot\nabla\left(\frac{1}{\Sigma_t}\Qgeneral\right)
\end{equation}
